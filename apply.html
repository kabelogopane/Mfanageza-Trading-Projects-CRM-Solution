How Mfanageza intends to use applicant information

Responsible party: Mfanageza Trading & Projects. The organisation's final registered/legal name, physical or postal address, and Information Officer contact details must be confirmed and published here before this form goes live.

Purpose: To receive and assess applications for skills-development and related programmes, contact applicants about their applications, manage programme administration, and meet applicable reporting or legal obligations.

Information: The live form is intended to collect contact details, province and town, education background, employment status, programme choice and information the applicant chooses to provide in the motivation field. Only information necessary for the stated purpose should be required.

Access and service providers: Access should be limited to authorised Mfanageza personnel. Salesforce may be used as the CRM service provider once the system, access controls and any applicable operator arrangements have been configured. Information must not be made public.

Voluntary information: Required fields are marked. If required information is not supplied, Mfanageza may be unable to assess the application. Optional fields may be left blank.

Retention: Records should be retained only for as long as needed for the application purpose or as required/authorised by law, under an approved retention schedule.

Your rights: Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction/deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, or raise a privacy complaint with the Information Regulator. The final live notice must provide the organisation's working privacy contact and the Regulator's current contact details.

Children: If an applicant is under 18, the organisation must apply the appropriate legal safeguards and obtain consent from a competent person where required before processing the child's personal information.
